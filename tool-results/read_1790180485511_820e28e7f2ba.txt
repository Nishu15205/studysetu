    62→
    63→## Introduction
    64→Real numbers combine every rational and irrational number into one family, and this chapter builds the number toolbox you will use all through Class 10 board maths. Two big ideas drive the exam: the **Fundamental Theorem of Arithmetic** (prime factorisation) and **irrationality proofs**.

    66→## Key Concepts

    69→For any two positive integers a and b, there exist unique integers q (quotient) and r (remainder) such that a = bq + r, with 0 ≤ r < b. It is the formal way of saying "divide and write the remainder". It powers the **Euclid division algorithm**, a step-by-step method to compute the HCF of two numbers: keep dividing the divisor by the remainder until the remainder becomes 0; the last non-zero remainder is the HCF.
    70→
    71→### Fundamental Theorem of Arithmetic
    72→Every composite number can be written as a product of primes, and this factorisation is unique apart from the order of factors. For example, 84 = 2 × 2 × 3 × 7, no matter how you start.     73→
    74→### HCF × LCM relation
    75→For any two positive integers a and b: HCF(a, b) × LCM(a, b) = a × b. Handy shortcut: find the HCF first, then divide a × b by it to get the LCM. Note this product rule is valid for **two** numbers only — for three numbers you must find each value separately.
    76→
    77→### Irrational numbers
    78→A number is irrational if it cannot be written as p/q with integers p, q (q ≠ 0). Their decimal expansions are non-terminating and non-repeating.    113→A chemical equation must obey the **law of conservation of mass**: atoms of each element must be equal on both sides.    201→
   202→## Introduction
   203→This chapter opens electrostatics: how charges behave at rest, the fields they create, and Gauss's law — the fastest tool for symmetric charge distributions. Boards regularly take 5-7 marks from here, including one derivation.
   204→
   205→## Key Concepts
   206→
   207→### Charge basics
   208→Charge is quantised: q = ne, with e = 1.6 × 10⁻¹⁹ C. It is conserved in every isolated system and additive with sign. Like charges repel, unlike attract. Conductors let charges flow; insulators hold them fixed; charging by induction moves charge without contact.
   209→
   210→### Coulomb's law
   211→F = k q₁q₂/r², with k = 1/(4πε₀) ≈ 9 × 10⁹ N m²/C². The force acts along the line joining the point charges, obeys Newton's third law, and is valid for point charges. In a medium of dielectric constant K, the force divides by K.
   212→
   213→### Electric field
   214→E = F/q₀ (force per unit positive test charge), unit N/C or V/m. Field of a point charge: E = kq/r², radially outward for positive q. Field lines start on + and end on − charges, never cross, and their density shows strength.
   215→
   216→### Electric dipole
   217→Two equal, opposite charges separated by 2a. Dipole moment p = q × 2a, directed from − to +. Axial field: E = 2kp/r³; equatorial field: E = kp/r³ (opposite to p). Torque in uniform field: τ = pE sinθ. Net force on a dipole in a uniform field is zero.
   218→
   219→### Gauss's law
   220→Total flux through a closed surface Φ = q(enclosed)/ε₀. Flux counts field lines; charges outside contribute zero net flux.    221→

   224→Each charge is 1.5 cm = 0.015 m away; fields are equal and opposite → E = 0. Point-charge symmetry kills the field at the centre of two equal like charges.

   228→- Coulomb force in medium drops by factor K.

   288→
   289→## Introduction
   290→This genetics chapter carries one of the highest weightages in Class 12 biology. It builds from Mendel's pea experiments to chromosome theory, linkage, and human genetic disorders — conceptual clarity here also powers the Molecular Basis of Inheritance chapter.
   291→
   292→## Key Concepts
   293→
   294→### Mendel's laws
   295→- **Law of Dominance**: in a monohybrid cross, only the dominant trait shows in F₁ (tall × dwarf → all tall).
   296→- **Law of Segregation**: alleles separate cleanly during gamete formation; F₂ shows 3 : 1 phenotype and 1 : 2 : 1 genotype. This is the only law without exceptions among Mendel's three.
   297→- **Law of Independent Assortment**: allele pairs for different traits separate independently in a dihybrid cross, giving the 9 : 3 : 3 : 1 F₂ ratio. Valid only for genes on different chromosomes or far apart on the same one.
   298→
   299→### Test cross & back cross
   300→Test cross mates a dominant-phenotype individual with the recessive parent type: a 1 : 1 ratio reveals a heterozygote. It is the standard "find the genotype" tool.
   301→
   302→### Incomplete & codominance
   303→- Incomplete dominance: heterozygote is intermediate (snapdragon: red × white → pink, F₂ 1 : 2 : 1).
   304→- Codominance: both alleles express together (AB blood group; ABO alleles Iᴬ, Iᴮ codominant, i recessive).
   305→
   306→### Chromosome theory & linkage
   307→Sutton and Boveri united Mendel's factors with chromosomes. Genes on the same chromosome show **linkage**; recombinant frequency depends on distance (closer = fewer recombinants). Drosophila linkage maps came from these ratios.
   308→
   309→### Sex determination & disorders
   310→Humans: XX female, XY male; the father's gamete decides the child's sex. Mendelian disorders include haemophilia (X-linked recessive), sickle-cell anaemia (autosomal recessive codominant), phenylketonuria. Chromosomal disorders: Down syndrome (trisomy 21), Turner's syndrome (XO female), Klinefelter's syndrome (XXY male).
   311→
   312→## Example
   313→Cross a carrier mother (XᴴXʰ) with a normal father (XᴴY) for haemophilia.
   314→Gametes: Xᴴ, Xʰ × Xᴴ, Y → daughters: ½ carrier; sons: ½ affected. So 25% of children (only boys) are expected haemophiliacs.
   315→
   316→## Quick Revision

   590→  for (const b of BOARDS) {
   591→    const row = await db.board.upsert({ where: { code: b.code }, create: b, update: b });
   592→    boards[b.code] = row.id;
   593→  }
   594→
   595→  // classes + subjects + chapters
   596→  const subjectIds: Record<string, string> = {}; // `${grade}-${slug}` -> id
   597→  const chapterIds: Record<string, { id: string; name: string }> = {}; // `${grade}-${slug}-${num}` -> id
   598→  for (const grade of [9, 10, 11, 12]) {
   599→    const cls = await db.classLevel.upsert({
   600→      where: { grade },
   601→      create: { grade, label: `Class ${grade}` },
   602→      update: { label: `Class ${grade}` },
   603→    });
   604→    for (const sub of CURRICULUM[grade]) {
   605→      const s = await db.subject.upsert({
   606→        where: { classId_slug: { classId: cls.id, slug: sub.slug } },
   607→        create: { name: sub.name, slug: sub.slug, classId: cls.id },
   608→        update: {},
   609→      });
   610→      subjectIds[`${grade}-${sub.slug}`] = s.id;
   611→      let num = 1;
   612→      for (const chName of sub.chapters) {
   613→        const ch = await db.chapter.upsert({
   614→          where: { subjectId_number: { subjectId: s.id, number: num } },
   615→          create: { number: num, name: chName, subjectId: s.id },
   616→          update: { name: chName },
   617→        });
   618→        chapterIds[`${grade}-${sub.slug}-${num}`] = { id: ch.id, name: chName };
   619→        num++;
   620→      }
   621→    }
   622→  }
   623→
   624→  // original seed notes
   625→  let noteCount = 0;
   626→  for (const [key, content] of Object.entries(SEED_NOTES)) {
   627→    const [grade, slug, num] = key.split("-");
   628→    const ch = chapterIds[`${grade}-${slug}-${num}`];
   629→    if (!ch) continue;
   630→    const keyPoints: string[] = [];
   631→    const revMatch = content.match(/## Quick Revision[\s\S]*?(?=\n## |$)/);
   632→    if (revMatch) {
   633→      keyPoints.push(...revMatch[0].split("\n").filter((l) => l.trim().startsWith("-")).map((l) => l.replace(/^[\s\-]+/, "").trim()).slice(0, 8));
   634→    }
   635→    const titleMatch = content.match(/^# (.+)$/m);
   636→    const words = content.split(/\s+/).length;
   637→    await db.note.upsert({
   638→      where: { chapterId: ch.id },
   639→      create: { title: titleMatch ? titleMatch[1] : `${ch.name} — Revision Notes`, content, keyPoints: JSON.stringify(keyPoints), readMins: Math.max(4, Math.round(words / 130)), chapterId: ch.id, origin: "original-seed" },
   640→      update: {},
   641→    });
   642→    noteCount++;
   643→  }
   644→
   645→  // original seed questions
   646→  let qCount = 0;
   647→  for (const q of SEED_QUESTIONS) {
   648→    const sid = subjectIds[`${q.grade}-${q.subject.toLowerCase().replace(/\s+/g, "-")}`];
   649→    if (!sid) continue;
   650→    const exists = await db.question.findFirst({ where: { text: q.text, subjectId: sid } });
   651→    if (exists) continue;
   652→    await db.question.create({
   653→      data: {
   654→        text: q.text, options: JSON.stringify(q.options), answer: q.answer, explanation: q.explanation,
   655→        difficulty: q.difficulty, marks: 1, subjectId: sid, chapterName: q.chapterName, origin: "original-seed",
   656→      },
   657→    });
   658→    qCount++;
   659→  }
   660→
   661→  // PYQ paper metadata with OFFICIAL board links only
   662→  const years = [2021, 2022, 2023, 2024, 2025];
   663→  const officialMap: Record<string, string> = {
   664→    CBSE: "https://cbse.gov.in/cbsenew/question-paper.html",
   665→    HBSE: "https://bseh.org.in/home?page=question-papers",
   666→    BSEB: "https://biharboardonline.bihar.gov.in",
   667→    KERALA: "https://dhsekerala.gov.in",
   668→  };
   669→  let paperCount = 0;
   670→  for (const b of BOARDS) {
   671→    for (const grade of [9, 10, 11, 12]) {
   672→      for (const sub of CURRICULUM[grade]) {
   673→        const sid = subjectIds[`${grade}-${sub.slug}`];
   674→        const gradeLabel = SUBJECT_FIX[sub.slug] || sub.name;
   675→        for (const y of years) {
   676→          await db.paper.upsert({
   677→            where: { boardId_classId_subjectId_year_kind: { boardId: boards[b.code], classId: (await db.classLevel.findUnique({ where: { grade } }))!.id, subjectId: sid, year: y, kind: "PYQ" } },
   678→            create: {
   679→              title: `${b.name} Class ${grade} ${gradeLabel} Question Paper ${y}`,
   680→              year: y, kind: "PYQ", officialUrl: officialMap[b.code],
   681→              note: "Open the official board portal to download the original paper. Use our original practice sets to test yourself.",
   682→              boardId: boards[b.code], classId: (await db.classLevel.findUnique({ where: { grade } }))!.id, subjectId: sid,
   683→            },
   684→            update: {},
   685→          }).catch(() => {});
   686→          paperCount++;
   687→        }
   688→      }
   689→    }
   690→    // CBSE official sample papers (2025) — linked, not stored
   691→    for (const grade of [10, 12]) {
   692→      for (const sub of CURRICULUM[grade]) {
   693→        const sid = subjectIds[`${grade}-${sub.slug}`];
   694→        const clsId = (await db.classLevel.findUnique({ where: { grade } }))!.id;
   695→        await db.paper.upsert({
   696→          where: { boardId_classId_subjectId_year_kind: { boardId: boards.CBSE, classId: clsId, subjectId: sid, year: 2025, kind: "SAMPLE" } },
   697→          create: {
   698→            title: `CBSE Class ${grade} ${SUBJECT_FIX[sub.slug] || sub.name} Official Sample Paper 2025-26`,
   699→            year: 2025, kind: "SAMPLE", officialUrl: "https://cbseacademic.nic.in/SQP_CLASSX_2025-26.html",
   700→            note: "Official sample paper & marking scheme published by CBSE Academic — always free and legal to download there.",
   701→            boardId: boards.CBSE, classId: clsId, subjectId: sid,
   702→          },
   703→          update: {},
   704→        }).catch(() => {});
   705→      }
   706→    }
   707→  }
   708→
   709→  // question of the day
   710→  const anyQ = await db.question.findFirst();
   711→  if (anyQ) {
   712→    await db.siteStat.upsert({ where: { key: "qotd" }, create: { key: "qotd", value: anyQ.id }, update: {} });
   713→  }
   714→
   715→  const totals = {
   716→    boards: await db.board.count(),
   717→    chapters: await db.chapter.count(),
   718→    notes: await db.note.count(),
   719→    questions: await db.question.count(),
   720→    papers: await db.paper.count(),
   721→  };
   722→  console.log(`Seeded: +${noteCount} notes, +${qCount} questions, ${paperCount} paper entries`, totals);
   723→}
   724→
   725→main().catch((e) => { console.error(e); process.exit(1); }).finally(() => db.$disconnect());
